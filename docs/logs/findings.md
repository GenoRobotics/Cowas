# Findings

Findings are `F<n>`. F1–F36 are in [stage 2 §4](../roadmap/stage2-pilots.md). F37 onward are here. Keep the numbers. Never delete a finding: change its status.

**Register.** This file is the register of findings. The status shown here is the current status. Log entries record what happened and link here; when a status changes, change it here and add the entry to the log.

**Severity:** Blocking > High > Medium > Low. **Step:** where the finding is resolved. **Status:** the latest known. **Recorded:** the date and the log entry where the finding is first mentioned. This is not always the day it was found; the entry gives the detail.

**Status words:** *open* (not fixed), *fixed in code* (not yet confirmed on the bench), *fixed* (confirmed), *closed*, *deferred*, *dropped*, *mitigated*.

**Area:** `firmware`, `host`, `hardware` (parts, wiring, sensors, power), `mechanical` (frame, manifold, seal, alignment).

**ID ranges used by parallel steps:** FW2 F60–F69, HO1 F70–F79, LA3 F80–F89, LA4 F110–F119.
