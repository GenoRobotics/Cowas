Two sensor additions

1. A second pressure sensor, after the filter

Why. Today we only measure pressure before the filter, so a clogging filter looks the same as a pinched tube or a closed valve. A sensor after the filter gives the pressure drop across the filter itself, which tells us:

  • when to stop a sample because the filter is clogged;
  • how clogged each filter was, recorded in the sample metadata;
  • when a filter is torn (no pressure drop) or the outlet is blocked.

Which sensor. ABPDANV060PGSA3: the same Honeywell family as our current ABPDANV150PGSA3 (water-compatible, SPI, 3.3 V, same code and wiring), but in the 0–60 psi range instead of 0–150 psi.

The clogging signal is the difference between two sensors, so their errors add up. With the 150 psi version, the error is more than 10 % of our 2.5 bar working range; with the 60 psi version, about 5 %. We suggest buying three: one after the filter, one to replace the current upstream sensor (same accuracy on both sides), and a spare.

Where and how. On a tee right after the filter outlet. It shares the SPI bus with the existing sensor (3.3 V, GND, MISO, SCK) and gets its own chip-select pin (D45). No extra electronics are needed. Its hose barb gets a clamp: the barb is where the current sensor popped out.

2. A second flow sensor, at the outlet

Why. With a flowmeter at the inlet and one at the outlet:

  • we record the volume that actually went through the filter (eDNA results are per litre);
  • we detect leaks: water going in but not coming out;
  • we confirm clogging: flow falls while the pressure drop rises.

Which sensor. The same model as the inlet meter, so the two can be compared directly. Before ordering, we check that its minimum flow is below our real sampling flow (measured with a cylinder). Small turbine meters under-read at low flow, which is exactly when a filter clogs.

Where and how. After the filter, mounted low so it stays full of water. Wiring: 5 V, GND, and the signal to pin D12 (or D36) with a pull-up to 3.3 V.

Specifications: Honeywell ABP Series datasheet (ranges, ±1.5 % total error band, 250 psi overpressure, liquid-media option).
