Sampling design
General
3 sampling sites out of 4 due to missing cone:
•	The lake shore at Bois Chamblard
•	The pond at Bois Chamblard
•	The canal (or river) at Bois Chamblard
•	On the L'eXPLORE platform[FS1.1]
The outflow of the pond at Bois Chamblard is the canal. The canal is approximatively 60-80m long and becomes underground 20m from the lake shore.
The experiment is split in two parts, one temporal and one spatial. The temporal part is performed on the canal at Bois Chamblard, sampling 2 times per day, one during daytime and one during nighttime. The initial plan was to repeat it over 2 days, but that would not leave any blank. The temporal part is thus reduced by one sampling point. The sequence will be: day, night, day. For the spatial side, 2 sampling locations are added, with 1 sampling event each. The only difference between the spatial samples and the temporal samples is that the three water samples are taking at the exact same location for temporal, and 5-10m apart for the spatial samples. 5-10m apart aims to estimate the local sample variability. 
The sampling spot at the lake shore at Bois Chamblard should be at least 50m away from the canal, and ideally the air sampler is between the lake and the first trees, not in between trees.
Air
Air samples are always taken over 8h.
Sun azimuth is at approx. 12h20, ideally the air sample is taken from –4h to +4h of azimuth for day/night comparison. Sunrise is at 7h08 and sunset is at 17h27. The sampling should start at 8h30 until 16h30. The sample during the night is then from 20h30 to 4h30. 
For comparability for the day samples from the temporal experiment, the air samples for the spatial experiment will be taken at the same time, but different days.
Water
If CoWaS not ready for field campaign, use a peristaltic pump and do the samples manually. Potential human contamination of the air sample...
Water – Time
The CoWaS is installed for the 48h temporal experiment at a fixed location. 
3 water samples are taken over the 8h air filtration period. One at the start, one at the middle and one at the end to be able to capture more fine-grained temporal dynamics in the water
Sample start times: 8h30, 12h00, 15h30. 1h approx sample time for Cowas.
During night, the water samples are at 20h30, 0h00, 3h30.
Water – Spatial
The timings will be the same as during the temporal experiment, but the sampling location will vary slightly spatially (about 5-10m).
The 3 local spatial replicates can be taken as fast as possible back-to-back.
Cowas sampling will from 11h to 14h (3 samples of approx 1hour) so that they are taken as quick as possible around the middle of the air sampling period.
After each sample the water intake needs to be moved by a few meters, so that the influence of small differences in sampling position can be assessed.
Preparation Protocol
1.	Replace all sterivex holder with straight tubes
2.	Put water intake into a 2L ethanol bottle
3.	Run 0.5 L slowly through the purge (pump power 15-20%)
4.	Run 100mL per sample slot
5.	Run micropump with Ethanol, and until no ethanol is left in the small tubes
6.	Run leftover ethanol through purge
7.	Place sterivex adapter + tubes in ethanol for 10 minutes
8.	Wipe prefilter holder with ethanol, put tubes into ethanol for 10 minutes
9.	Wipe Coriolis Air sampler with Ethanol
10.	Wipe DNAshield Falcon tube and both lids. Close with original lid, and place drilled one into fresh ziploc.
11.	Remove straight tubes from manifold and add desinfected sterivex holder.
12.	Charge air sampler
13.	Label  ziplocs
14.	Packs all things from list above
15.	Pack all camping gear + food for 1 night and 2 days
Sample protocol
Day 1 (date: 27 October)
1.	Choose exact sampling location for temporal samples[FS2.1]
2.	Setup sampling equipment
a.	Place CoWaS on palette, cover with XX
b.	As close as possible, put up tripod and slip in Air sampler with tripod adapter
3.	Add DNA shield into Falcontube, and close with drilled lid
4.	Ensure CoWaS is correctly in place.
5.	Load DNA shield until checkvalve
6.	Insert cone to air sampler
7.	Start air sampler at 8h30, and put alarm 8h after
8.	Keep ~50m distance to air sampler to avoid contamination, come closer only if CoWaS has technical issues.
9.	The 3 water samples are taken autonomously
10.	After 8h (around 16h30) stop the air sampler and put cone in zip lock.
Set up of campsite, check thoroughly if CoWaS did work as expected
11.	Ensure everything is ready for the night sampling
12.	Start air sampler at 20h30, and put alarm 8h after or configure timer on sampler
13.	Sleep if confident enough that the CoWaS will work
14.	Stop air sampler, and put cone in zip lock
15.	Check CoWaS for anomalies

Day 2 (28.10)
Repeat 6-10 during day. Then proceed to Negative Controls.
Water Field Negative Control:
16.	Quickly wipe end of water intake tubing from outside with ethanol
17.	Insert water intake into Milli-Q water
18.	Run control sample sequence:
a.	Purge 2-3L (instead of 6, as it would be too much Milli-Q to source)
b.	Filter 1L through sterivex (or less if it is detected as clogged)
c.	Same steps as with regular sampling
Air Field Negative Control:
19.	Open sterilised cone
20.	Insert it into the air sampler
21.	Wait 1 minutes (time to start sample)
22.	Remove cone from sampler and put into labeled ziploc
Skipped due to missing cone: Repeat steps 11-14 for a second night sampling.
Packing:
23.	Check CoWaS that it took all samples, and check for potential debug messages
24.	Turn off CoWaS
25.	Take manifold out and take samples, screw caps on and put in labelled ziplocs
26.	Store samples at room temperature
The sterivex should be almost entirely filled with DNA shield (volume: 2.2 mL)
Water samples should be processed within a month.
