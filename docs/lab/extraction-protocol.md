# eDNA extraction from Sterivex filters (Zymo Quick-DNA Miniprep Plus)

The DNA extraction protocol used in the lab for the filters of the 2026 field work (Bois Chamblard and LéXPLORE, from both the CoWaS and the manual sampling). It is an adaptation of the Zymo Miniprep Plus kit for filtered eDNA. Extra heating and vortexing steps give yields comparable to more expensive kits. The steps below follow the source protocol; the notes for the CoWaS filters are in §6.

**Source:** Eldridge Wisely (2025), *eDNA Extraction using Zymo Miniprep plus kit*, V.2, protocols.io, protocol ID 227632, created 18 Sep 2025, status "working" (draft, no DOI). University of California, San Diego. Distributed under the [Creative Commons Attribution License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). ## 1. Materials

| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| Zymo Quick-DNA Miniprep Plus kit | Proteinase K, Genomic Binding Buffer, DNA Pre-Wash Buffer, g-DNA Wash Buffer, DNA Elution Buffer, spin columns, collection tubes |
| Zymo DNA/RNA Shield | Optional (for the negative control and samples without it) |
| 5 mL centrifuge tubes, sterile | For disk filters and the negative control |
| 1.5 mL tubes | Caps cut off to hold the spin columns at elution |
| 1.5 mL low-binding tubes (Axygen) | Final eluate |
| **Sterivex only:** 1.7 or 2 mL microcentrifuge tubes, 50 mL centrifuge tubes, a centrifuge that takes 50 mL tubes | Recovery of the lysate from the Sterivex |
| **Qubit only:** Qubit tubes, 1× HS dsDNA buffer and standards, Qubit reader | Quantification |
| Hula mixer or rotating incubator at 55 °C, vortex, microcentrifuge (≥ 12,000 × g) | |

## 2. Preparation

1. Clean the extraction area with bleach or DNA Away, then UV for at least 15 min if possible.
2. Turn on the hula mixer / incubator: 55 °C, 124 rpm.
3. If the Proteinase K is not yet prepared: add 1040 µL of buffer to the Proteinase K powder of the kit.
4. **Extraction negative control:** if the samples are stored in 400 µL of DNA/RNA Shield (vials, tubes or Sterivex), put 400 µL of DNA/RNA Shield in the negative control's 5 mL tube.
5. If the samples were not stored in 400 µL of DNA/RNA Shield: add to every sample *and* to the negative control either 400 µL of DNA/RNA Shield, or 200 µL of Biofluid and Cell Buffer (red) plus 200 µL of DNA Elution Buffer.
6. **Sterivex only:** if the filters hold a lot of sediment, spin them down with their caps on, 3000 rpm, 1 min, room temperature.

## 3. Lysis

7. Add 20 µL of Proteinase K to every sample (Sterivex or 5 mL tube) and to the negative control.
8. Vortex 5 min at room temperature, speed setting 4.
9. Incubate 15 min at 55 °C, tubes on their sides, in the hula mixer at 124 rpm.
10. Vortex again 5 min at room temperature, speed setting 4.
11. Put the DNA Elution Buffer in the incubator (55 °C) when the samples come out.
12. Prepare and label the spin columns.
13. Add 420 µL of Genomic Binding Buffer to each sample. For a Sterivex, pipette into the inflow so that the tip touches the filter.
14. Vortex again 5 min at room temperature, speed setting 4.

## 4. Lysate recovery from the Sterivex (Sterivex only)

For each filter: one 50 mL centrifuge tube and one 1.7 or 2 mL microcentrifuge tube.

15. Snap off the outflow tip of the Sterivex and put the outflow into the microcentrifuge tube. Remove the inflow cap. Put the two together into the 50 mL tube.
16. Cap the 50 mL tube and centrifuge 3000 rpm, 1 min, room temperature. If not all the lysate has come through, centrifuge again.

## 5. Binding, washing and elution

17. Transfer 840 µL of lysate to the spin column.
18. Centrifuge at maximum speed (≥ 12,000 × g), 1 min.
19. Optional: spin the Sterivex or 5 mL tubes briefly (5–10 s) to collect more lysate. Empty the collection tube into the liquid waste and put the spin column back in the same collection tube.
20. Repeat (up to 840 µL each time) until all the lysate has gone through the column.
21. Put the spin columns in new collection tubes. Add 400 µL of DNA Pre-Wash Buffer.
    - *Qubit:* bring the Qubit buffer and standards to room temperature now.
22. Centrifuge at maximum speed, 1 min; empty the collection tube.
23. Add 700 µL of g-DNA Wash Buffer.
24. Centrifuge at maximum speed, 1 min; empty the collection tube.
25. Add 200 µL of g-DNA Wash Buffer.
26. Centrifuge at maximum speed, 1 min. Cut the caps off 1.5 mL tubes to hold the spin columns in the next step.
27. Put the spin columns in the cap-less 1.5 mL tubes.
28. Add 100 µL of warm (55 °C) DNA Elution Buffer directly onto the column's filter.
29. Leave 10 min at room temperature.
30. Label the final 1.5 mL low-binding tubes.
31. Centrifuge at maximum speed, 1 min.
32. Transfer the eluate to the labelled low-binding tube.
33. Store the eluate at −20 °C, after the 2 µL for the Qubit are taken (step 36).

## 5b. Quantification (Qubit 1× HS dsDNA)

34. Put Qubit tubes in a clean, empty 1000 µL tip box: one per sample, plus two for the standards.
35. Add 198 µL of 1× HS Qubit buffer to each sample tube and 190 µL to each standard tube (protect from light).
36. Add 2 µL of eluate to each sample tube; close and label.
37. Add 10 µL of each standard to its tube; close and label.
38. Close tightly and vortex 10 s; no bubbles at the bottom. Leave in the dark for at least 2 min and at most 30 min.
39. Plug in the Qubit reader and select the assay on the buffer's label (1× HS dsDNA).
40. Read standard 1, then standard 2.
41. Run the samples: volume of extract 2 µL, units ng/µL.
42. Write the concentrations in the lab notebook (and in the sample list, §6).

## 6. Notes for the CoWaS filters

These are open points for the lab, not changes to the protocol.

- **DNA/RNA Shield volume: more than the protocol, and variable** (confirmed by the user, 2026-11-12). The protocol assumes 400 µL of DNA/RNA Shield per filter (step 4). The CoWaS filters held more than that, and the amount varied from sample to sample, most likely with the clogging of the filter (F255). The CoWaS doses by time, not by volume (about 2.2 mL per filter at LéXPLORE, field protocol kit list). The manual filters got 1 mL. So the lysis chemistry differs between samples: record per batch how the shield in the Sterivex was handled (volume kept, removed or topped up) and match the negative control to it. The measured dose of stage 2 B7 and the lab work of C2 will give the real volume per filter.
- **Residual water.** A CoWaS Sterivex is emptied by air, but a wet membrane keeps some water (F28, [shield-protocol.md](shield-protocol.md)); it dilutes the shield before extraction.
- **Controls.** One extraction negative control per extraction batch (step 4), in addition to the field blanks of each deployment (the CoWaS equipment blanks and the manual blank). Record which samples share a batch.
- **Traceability.** Link each eluate to its sample label in the [sample list](../reports/lexplore-sample-list-2026-11.xlsx): extraction date, batch, negative control, Qubit concentration. Flag the samples already flagged in the field (E1_T7_20M_R1, expired filter, F253; E2_T7_05M_R1, shield added by hand; the E1_T5_01M_R1 record is not a sample, F244).
